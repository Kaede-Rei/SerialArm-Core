Kudu Calm Control theme tokens
Source: https://github.com/AdventDevInc/kudu/tree/4fb0dbdc51f65362b966d327c781dd2a874e0f00
The semantic token structure in apps/launcher/renderer/styles.css adapts the final Calm Control definitions in src/renderer/src/globals.css. SerialArm retains its existing Proof-derived warm-neutral surfaces and ink, with a user-selected WeChat-green brand and brighter status palette. SerialArm aliases and semantic visualization mappings are adaptations; the current palette is not an unmodified Kudu theme.
